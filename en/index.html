ODDDAY describes data practices separately for each app and website. Please refer to the relevant service below. Before launching a new service, ODDDAY will add its actual data-handling details.

ODDDAY processes information necessary to provide its services. What is collected, where records are stored, how they are used, and how they can be deleted depend on the specific service. Device-only records and data sent to external servers are not the same across all services. This general notice does not substitute for disclosing a service's actual use of personal data or third-party SDKs.

2. Covered services

DAY.zip (Android and Web)

DAY.zip stores entered dates, titles, notes, and card settings on the device or in the browser. Under its currently published policy, it does not require an account or synchronize records to the developer's server, and its Android app has no advertising or analytics SDK. Please consult the service-specific policy for backup files, widgets, website hosting, and deletion procedures.
